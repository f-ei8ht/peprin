## Export

- **MP4 export** via WebCodecs H.264 + AAC encoding and mediabunny muxing
- **WebM export** via VP9 + Opus
- **Animated GIF export** with a built-in LZW encoder
- **PNG sequence export** for frame-by-frame workflows
- Progress indicator with frame count and estimated time remaining
- Cancel support with immediate resource cleanup
- Configurable resolution, frame rate, bitrate, and quality
- Audio track mixing via OfflineAudioContext during export
- Descriptive errors when a codec is unsupported by the browser

## Static pages

- Blog with markdown rendering from `content/blog/`
- Changelog with date-filtered entries
- Contributors, Roadmap, Sponsors, and Brand asset pages
- RSS feed for blog and changelog updates
- Sitemap and robots.txt covering all public routes
